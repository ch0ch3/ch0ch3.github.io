# Design Context

## Direction

- Automotive.
- Professional.
- Clean.
- High legibility.
- Responsive from 320 px.

## Core palette

- Black or near-black.
- White or warm white.
- Provisional red.
- Neutral grays.

Final brand color values are pending confirmation from the identity manual.

## Layout direction

- Light Header.
- Brand at the left.
- Navigation at the right on desktop.
- Compact, always-visible navigation on mobile.
- Hero divided between dark content and an intentional CSS typographic/geometric composition until official photography is available.
- Content above the composition on mobile.
- Rectangular red primary actions with clear secondary contrast.
- Gapless service grid with six confirmed offerings.
- Condensed-looking headings using safe local font stacks.

## Constraints

- No external fonts.
- No invented assets.
- No external images.
- No loud gradients.
- No glassmorphism.
- No neon effects.
- No 3D effects.
- No decorative animation.
- No unnecessary visual effects.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Maintain accessible contrast and visible focus.
- Minimum 44 px touch targets.
